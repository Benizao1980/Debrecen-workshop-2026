# Instructor answer key

## Exercise 0

- Species: can be strongly inferred from genomic sequence; PubMLST/rMLST can assist.
- MLST ST: directly determined from the allelic profile when exact alleles/profile are defined.
- Direct chicken-to-bird transmission: **not established by the genome alone**.
- Resemblance to source populations: probabilistic inference.
- Known resistance determinant: can be detected directly from sequence, but genotype-to-phenotype interpretation still has assumptions.
- Farm antibiotic use caused the determinant: cannot be established from sequence alone.

### A - Cj_2849

Songthrush from the non-agricultural Ottenby wild-bird collection. ST1347, CC1347, hBAPS13. This is the cleanest contrast with farm-associated generalist lineages in the project tree.

**Teaching point:** a genome can sit convincingly in a wild-bird-associated part of a sampled population without proving that every member of that lineage is ecologically restricted to wild birds.

### B - Cj_10787

Crow sampled near farm 2. ST45, CC45, hBAPS10.

**Teaching point:** CC45 is a classic generalist problem. The study contains ST45 across agricultural and wildlife contexts. A source classifier may be less decisive because biological host switching erodes source structure.

### C - Cj_10726

Crow sampled near farm 6. ST686, CC682, hBAPS8.

**Teaching point:** not every near-farm bird carries a CC45-like lineage. Farm proximity and bacterial lineage are related questions, not synonyms.

### D - Cj_10753

Barn swallow sampled near farm 5. ST1525, CC unassigned, hBAPS12. ST1525 occurred in both organic chicken and wild-bird groups in the study.

**Teaching point:** sharing an ST across hosts is a useful signal, but a seven-locus match is far too coarse to claim recent host-to-host transmission.

## Genome Comparator

Do not give participants a universal allele-distance threshold for transmission. The threshold depends on organism, scheme, time scale, recombination, QC and epidemiological context.

## Source attribution

The correct answer is not simply whichever class has the maximum probability. Participants should discuss whether the probability is high, whether the lineage is a known generalist, and whether the reference panel represents the ecological/geographic possibilities.

## AMR

The strongest project-level teaching contrast is **near-farm vs non-agricultural wild birds**, not “wild birds are resistant”. Keep lineage structure in the discussion: AMR may move by clonal expansion, host movement and/or horizontal gene transfer.
